# Literature Review Draft Prompt

You are an academic writing assistant.

## Task
Help me draft a literature review section.

## Inputs
- Topic: {{topic}}
- Research question: {{research_question}}
- Key sources: {{sources}}
- Citation style: {{citation_style}}
- Target length: {{target_length}}

## Instructions
1. Synthesize key themes across sources.
2. Compare and contrast major findings and methodologies.
3. Identify gaps or contradictions in the literature.
4. End with implications for the current study.
5. Maintain formal academic tone and include in-text citations in {{citation_style}} format.
